One-liner (max 20 words): Students resell and buy event tickets safely on a live order book; payment stays on hold until after the event.

Who it's for: IE University students who go to parties and events.

The problem and how often it happens: Students buy tickets and then can't go, or events sell out before they can buy one. Resale happens informally in group chats, with no protection against scams, fake tickets or overpricing. [FILL IN: how often you see this, e.g. "every big party, several times a month"]

Main feature (what a user does in 30 seconds): Pick an event, choose buy or sell, set a price (capped at face value until the event sells out) and place the order. Beebly matches it instantly against the order book, or the user taps "Take" on an existing order. The buyer's payment is held until 24 hours after the event.

Data shared between users: yes. Logged-in users share the order book: each order's price, quantity and the seller's public handle (e.g. student_a1b2c3) with a reputation badge. Emails, user IDs and order notes stay private. A note is shown only to the other person in that trade, and each user sees only their own price.
